# Warranty and recalls

## Life support class hardware

Oxygen generation, air scrubbing, and water recycling units are life support
class. They have a 4 hour replacement service level agreement. Any ticket that
reports a life support failure is paged to the on-call engineer immediately, and
support says so in the reply.

## Open recalls

When the order record names an open recall, support states the recall id and the
fault, and arranges the remedy the record lists. If a replacement unit is in
stock at a depot the customer can reach, emergency dispatch is offered in the
first reply. Directing a customer to the recall page is not a remedy.
