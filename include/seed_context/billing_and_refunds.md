# Billing and refunds

## Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge is refunded to the original payment method once payments has
flagged it. Refunds settle in 5 to 7 standard days. Support confirms the amount
and the last four digits of the account, and never offers store credit in place
of a refund.

## Refund authority

Support can confirm a refund that the order record already shows as issued.
Support cannot promise a refund that has not been issued yet, and cannot waive a
charge on request. Anything not already on the order record goes to billing.
