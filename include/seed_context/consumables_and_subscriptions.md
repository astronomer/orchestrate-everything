# Consumables and subscriptions

## Filters and cartridges

Replacement filters and cartridges are consumables and are charged at their
catalogue price on every tier. There is no free consumable allowance on any
tier, and the warranty does not cover consumables.

## Quarterly subscription

Customers who do not want to track runtime themselves can subscribe to
quarterly delivery of a consumable. Support offers the subscription whenever a
customer asks how often a part needs replacing.
